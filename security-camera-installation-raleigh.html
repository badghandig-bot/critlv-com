Homes and exterior locations

Plan mounting points and cable routes around the property. Exterior walls, rooflines, attic access and the recorder location help determine the practical installation approach.

Existing-system changes

Coverage and infrastructure belong in one scope.

A site review considers the intended view, mounting position, lighting conditions, cable pathway and network equipment. The proposal identifies the camera count, supplied equipment, installation responsibilities and any existing-system troubleshooting that is included.

Camera wiring, network ports and recorder setup need to work together. If the project needs additional data runs, a patch panel or closet cleanup, we can scope those requirements alongside the camera installation. Confirm ongoing support and monitoring arrangements separately.

Planning questions

Before the work starts.

Can we supply our own cameras?

Send the camera, recorder and network-equipment model numbers. Compatibility, missing components, installation scope and warranty responsibilities need to be confirmed before the work is accepted.

Can existing camera wiring be reused?

Possibly. Its type, condition and suitability for the planned equipment need to be reviewed. Replacing a camera alone does not resolve an underlying cable or power problem.

Do you handle indoor and outdoor installations?

We review both indoor and outdoor camera projects. Mounting surfaces, access, equipment suitability and cable routing help determine the installation scope.

What should I send for a camera quote?

Send the site address, desired camera count and views, proposed mounting locations, existing equipment models, and your preferred schedule. Photos of the mounting areas and recorder or network closet are useful.
