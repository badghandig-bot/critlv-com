Privacy & analytics choices

Updated November 25, 2026

Your quote request

When you contact CRIT Networking, we use the details you provide to respond, understand the project, prepare a quote, and arrange service. Website quote requests go to our client-management system at app.critlv.com. Your contact details, project description, and optional answers are part of that request.

The optional “How did you hear about CRIT?” answer helps us understand which marketing brings useful inquiries. A broad source label, an approved campaign name, and the public page where your visit began may also accompany the quote you choose to submit. This first-party request context works separately from Google Analytics. Choosing “Keep analytics off” or using a browser privacy signal turns automatic source capture off too. We do not attach your full browsing history.

Optional website analytics

If Google Analytics is enabled on this site, it loads only after you allow optional analytics. It measures public page visits, broad traffic sources, clicks on phone or email links, and quote-form activity. A submitted lead is counted only after our system accepts the quote request. Clicking a phone or email link does not tell us whether a call or email was completed.

Our analytics events do not include your name, email, phone number, project address, project description, or coupon code. We exclude query strings and URL fragments from page addresses sent to analytics. We do not use this implementation for advertising personalization.

Your analytics choice is remembered in this browser when storage is available. If you allow analytics, Google Analytics may use its own cookies to measure visits. Unless you turn automatic source capture off, a short-lived first-source record is kept for this browsing session; it expires after inactivity or after one day. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals keep optional analytics off.

You can change your choice using . Turning it off stops further optional collection from this page; it does not erase data previously collected. You can also clear this site's browser storage and cookies.

Services used by the website

Cloudflare hosts the website. Google services may deliver fonts, maps, and optional analytics. Videos and other external links open services with their own privacy practices. We use our client portal to manage requests and the communication needed to provide service.
